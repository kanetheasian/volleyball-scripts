# BVL v0.8.53: dump landing bounds and Spike recovery

The reported long dumps were native Set fallbacks whose planned downward tilt did not match the neutral launch feedback. The predicted flight landed inside the baseline while confirmed launches carried farther. Emergency delivery now uses the matching standing/gliding contact-height cohort and excludes unmeasured Set tilt. It validates both the nominal landing and a more conservative flight-distance estimate before selecting a dump. Known out-of-bounds candidates are no longer returned as mandatory best-effort shots; an in-bounds recovery can be chosen instead of insisting on an unreachable deep corner.

Spike dumps retain P15 preference and measured contact, net-clearance and defender checks. Their target and final validation margins now reserve six studs inside court boundaries, after a live spike drifted from a barely accepted predicted lane toward the sideline. Ordinary spiking aim and normal hitter-set placement were not retuned.

The current kit initially had five saved tilted, high-contact spike examples and no neutral examples matching the reported receives. Two user-performed neutral P15 spikes filled that gap. Subsequent automatic native Spikes were recorded and persisted through the existing matching-kit calibration cache. Missing or incompatible contact samples can still require a safe Set fallback; a saved sample count alone does not establish that the present contact is supported.

The final user-driven check recorded three confirmed, nearly neutral P15 Spike dumps (ball IDs 2848, 2850 and 2851), each followed by an opponent-court floor landing. Earlier candidate attempts included Set fallbacks intercepted by receivers and a Spike landing near the sideline; those are retained in local evidence. Unrequested approaches were not independently counted in this user-driven check, and the three confirmed actions do not establish an overall completion rate or reliability at other positions. No bot feeds or player movement were automated for this test.

Validation: 1,769 main/component assertions, 43 preview/viewer runtime assertions, 11 Rayfield compatibility checks, extension compilation, and full-bundle compilation at optimization levels 0 and 1 passed. New regressions use recorded native launch values and cover unmeasured tilt rejection, glide propagation, out-only refusal, conservative flight bounds, mirrored/rotated courts, and the near-sideline Spike case. Live runtime errors were empty. Preview-only Xeno mode and the existing loader remain available.
