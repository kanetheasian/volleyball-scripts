# BVL v0.8.54: Spike dump selection with receivers present

Ground receivers' possible running coverage now ranks legal Spike lanes instead of rejecting every lane they might reach. The planner continues to reject a trajectory intersecting a predicted occupied receiver region, a block, the net, an antenna, or an out-of-bounds landing. Final swing validation applies the same distinction. The existing six-stud landing reserve and measured contact/kit/phase restrictions remain in effect.

The search adds targets between receivers and near court edges, then evaluates candidates near the setter's current facing first. A deadline check on each target bounds crowded-court work, and repeated forecasts stop correcting negligible bearing error. Potential receiver overlap is exposed separately in the rejection trace. A selected least-covered shot is not labeled an open court if predicted running coverage remains. Opponent receivers no longer cover points entirely on the setter's side of the net.

The prior release fails a three-receiver fixture that this release passes. New regressions also verify choosing the less-covered side, rejecting an occupied receiver lane at final validation, rejecting an actual block, retaining an early candidate under a simulated work limit, and ignoring receiver coverage wholly before the net crossing. All 1,777 main/component assertions, 43 preview/viewer runtime assertions, 11 Rayfield compatibility checks and full-bundle compilation at optimization levels 0 and 1 passed.

Live evidence is limited to a passive audit of v0.8.53 with players present. One observed approach included matching-contact samples but hit the search limit while evaluating net-colliding candidates; as the ball fell, its later contact heights no longer matched the saved samples. This was not proof that every failure came from defender coverage. The user was unable to perform a new test, so v0.8.54 was not loaded into or tested in that match. No character movement, feeds or settings were automated. Player-dependent behavior after this update remains pending live confirmation; some contacts can still need the Set fallback when no measured legal Spike exists.
